Bundled reference data: attribution and reuse review
Last updated on: 2026-09-10

Scope: the eight data files shipped in ldsc/data. Software licensing and
dataset reuse terms are assessed separately. This record grants no new
rights in third-party datasets. No data values were changed in this review.

1. hm3_curated_map.tsv.gz

This project's curated HapMap3 map contains dual-build positions, alleles,
genetic-map positions, and frequency information. Git records its addition
at a190b50 (2026-05-10) and an allele-aware update at 5861b2a (2026-05-15).
The repository does not establish the original source release and reuse
terms for every column. Attribution and redistribution verification remain
incomplete; the maintainer needs the original curation inputs and records.
Do not label the complete table GPL, CC0, or public domain based solely on
its inclusion in this software package or on the name HapMap3.

docs/current/bundled-hm3-map-coordinate-validation.md records checks against
UCSC dbSNP 151 common tracks, Ensembl, and NCBI. Those checks establish
coordinate evidence, not the complete table's source lineage or license.
If original inputs are from IGSR/1000 Genomes, verify their dataset-specific
reuse policy and requested attribution against the exact release:
https://www.internationalgenome.org/faq/do-i-need-permission-to-use-igsr-data-in-my-own-scientific-research/
This source is a policy reference, not confirmation that every bundled
column originated in that collection.

2. hm3_chr_pos_reference.tsv.gz

Derived here from hm3_curated_map.tsv.gz by
tools/hm3/build_hm3_chr_pos_reference.py. The selection procedure is recorded
in readme.txt. This subset inherits the unresolved source-attribution and
reuse questions above; subsetting does not establish new upstream terms.

3. regions/centromeres_core.hg19.bed and centromeres_core.hg38.bed

Derived by tools/regions/build_region_beds.py from UCSC Genome Browser data:
  hg19 gap track, rows with type=centromere:
  https://api.genome.ucsc.edu/getData/track?genome=hg19;track=gap
  hg38 centromeres track, merged to one span per chromosome:
  https://api.genome.ucsc.edu/getData/track?genome=hg38;track=centromeres
  https://genome.ucsc.edu/cgi-bin/hgTrackUi?db=hg38&g=centromeres

Credit UCSC and the source assembly/track producers; consult the track
description for its source publications. Region bundling began 2026-06-06
(a4f217e); the pericentromeric presets were revised 2026-06-12 (2081c40).
The inspected UCSC data policy permits public and commercial use from
UCSC's perspective, while noting that original data producers can impose
additional terms. This is distinct from UCSC software licensing:
https://genome.ucsc.edu/license/
An exact source snapshot date was not retained in the BED headers. General
UCSC policy was verified; a complete release-specific rights chain was not.

4. regions/centromeres.hg19.bed and centromeres.hg38.bed

Derived from the core intervals above, padded by +/-3 cM using the
Alkes-group maps genetic_map_hg19_withX.txt and genetic_map_hg38_withX.txt.
Credit the UCSC track producers and the genetic-map producers. The method
follows the pericentromeric exclusion described in Bulik-Sullivan et al.
(2015), DOI 10.1038/ng.3211. Source descriptions and derivation are in
tools/regions/build_region_beds.py and
docs/current/region-exclusion-presets.md, "Source of the coordinates".

The genetic maps themselves are not bundled. Their exact release, original
download URL, and data-specific redistribution terms are not recorded in
this repository. The Alkes-group label alone is insufficient to establish
them. Verification of terms for these derived intervals remains open; a
license for map-processing software would not settle data reuse terms.

5. regions/mhc.hg19.bed and mhc.hg38.bed

Project-maintained constants: chromosome 6, BED interval [25000000,35000000)
for each build. These files are generated from MHC constants in
tools/regions/build_region_beds.py, rather than a bundled external interval
table. The broad build-consistent presets were recorded in 15d6eea
(2026-06-12). Their BED headers and the region-presets document preserve
the scientific convention and coordinate basis.

Completion requires original HM3 curation records and exact genetic-map
sources/releases/terms. Ask the maintainer, Wenbin Wu (drkwu999@gmail.com),
to record those sources before treating resource licensing as fully verified.
